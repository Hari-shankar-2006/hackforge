Doing something wild.

Mrs. Sharma, a dedicated teacher, spends hours every week preparing
lessons—researching resources, organizing activities, and ensuring content is
engaging. This leaves her with little time to focus on individual student needs or
improving her teaching methods. An AI-powered lesson planning tool could transform
this process. By automatically generating dynamic lesson plans with diverse teaching
methods and resources, the tool would save teachers valuable time while enhancing
the learning experience for students.
